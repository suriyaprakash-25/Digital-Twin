# DrivePortz Pilot — Garage Guide

## Daily workflow
1. Sign in with the GARAGE pilot account.
2. Confirm your garage profile, services, availability and contact details are correct.
3. Review new bookings promptly.
4. Keep booking/service statuses current so customers and pilot analytics reflect reality.
5. Complete service verification only for work assigned to your verified garage.
6. Upload bills/service evidence that belongs to the correct booking/service.
7. Review payment/earnings records before reporting a mismatch.

## Booking handling
- Accept/reject only bookings your garage can actually service.
- Do not mark work completed before the service record and final bill are accurate.
- For a stuck or incorrect booking, use **Feedback → Booking Issue**.

## Payment and settlement handling
- Customer payments and garage settlement are different workflows.
- During the controlled pilot, automated live payout jobs remain disabled unless explicitly enabled after production validation.
- If a payment/earning amount looks wrong, use **Feedback → Payment Issue** and include invoice/payment references.
- Do not request customer card credentials or OTPs.

## Complaints and disputes
If a customer raises a complaint:
- Keep communication factual.
- Preserve booking, service and bill records.
- Do not delete or alter evidence to “fix” the complaint.
- Use the DrivePortz dispute/support path and cooperate with the pilot admin.

## Feedback
Use the in-app Feedback button for:
- Bug Report
- Performance Issue
- Payment Issue
- Booking Issue
- Account Issue
- Feature Request

## Security
Use a unique password, keep staff access controlled, and never share the production admin account or API/payment credentials.
