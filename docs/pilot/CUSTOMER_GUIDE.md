# DrivePortz Pilot — Customer Guide

## Start here
1. Sign in with your pilot account.
2. Add your vehicle and verify the registration/vehicle details you enter.
3. Use the Marketplace to choose a garage/service where available.
4. Create a booking and watch its status in DrivePortz.
5. Review service/invoice details before making a payment.
6. Keep your service, insurance and vehicle documents limited to files relevant to DrivePortz.

## Payments
- Use only the DrivePortz payment flow shown in the application.
- Do not pay twice because a page appears slow. Check Payment History first.
- If money is debited but DrivePortz does not show a successful payment, submit **Feedback → Payment Issue** and include the booking/invoice reference. Do not share full card credentials.

## Booking/service issues
For a rejected, stuck or incorrect booking, submit **Feedback → Booking Issue**. Include the garage name, booking date and what you expected to happen.

## AI features
Vehicle Doctor/CoPilot are assistance tools, not a replacement for a qualified mechanic or emergency help. If the vehicle may be unsafe to drive, stop safely and get professional help instead of waiting for an AI response.

## Report a bug or suggestion
Use the floating **Feedback** button:
- **Bug Report** for something broken.
- **Feature Request** for an improvement.
- **Account Issue** for login/profile/access problems.
- **Garage Complaint** for a service-partner complaint.

Screenshots help, but remove unrelated personal information before uploading.

## Privacy and terms
Read the current **Privacy & Data Handling Notice** and **Terms of Service** from the public DrivePortz pages. Only upload documents you are authorized to provide.

## What to include in a support report
- What you were trying to do.
- What actually happened.
- Booking/payment/vehicle reference if relevant.
- Screenshot if useful.
- Whether the issue is still blocking you.
